Architecture Before the Formula

Studies neural architecture before collapsing a represented computation to its composite input-output function. The paper separates preserved predecessor information, represented organization, and continuation accessibility, then studies how architectural distinctions can change under composition with different upstream representations.

Mechanistic interpretability · 2026

Mechanistic Localization Is Distribution-Relative

Shows that a fixed network and fixed receiver support need not determine a single receiver-level rule. Changing the realized probability law can change the rule while leaving structural support unchanged, separating predictive adequacy from context invariance.

Scientific machine learning · 2026

From Detection to Identity

A public case study in 3D cell-lineage inference. The project separates local visual evidence from accepted biological identity, missing annotation from negative supervision, motion accuracy from uncertainty calibration, and local relation scores from globally compatible lineage structure.
